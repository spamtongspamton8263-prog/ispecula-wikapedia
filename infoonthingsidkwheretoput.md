## **arcanic energy**
Arcanic Energy is a fundamental, high-level force in the Ispecula-verse, distinct from raw magical or physical power. It is visualized as arcane sigils, geometric shapes, or symbols that appear when the power is in use. It is described as extremely rare and difficult to manage, often at the cost of the user's mental well-being.

The appearance and application of Arcanic Energy are highly personalized:

· 赤い満月 (Red Full Moon): Uses refined geometric seals with precise, severe effects, such as erasure or time manipulation, utilizing a red-and-black energy signature.
· Ziron (Architect of Emergence): Infuses his kinetic combat style (lever-action shotgun and knife) with Arcanic Energy. He exhibits an "ice blue" signature and can perform :-style techniques like 赤い満月 but with an "emergence" twist.
· General Properties: The energy can take the form of stars, cubes, or other abstract constructs. It is incredibly draining and mentally taxing to wield, making it a high-risk, high-reward power source.

Arcanic Energy is not tied to physical bodies, making it effective against enemies without conventional biology, and is wielded by specific entities like God of Nothingness (Oblivion) and others, though the Architects (creator beings) tend to avoid using it in direct combat.

## EYEPATCH MODES IN DETAIL

🔷 TIME MODE

· Symbol: Dark blue clock
· Abilities:
  · Time Stop – Stops time in a local area for up to 10 seconds. Ispecula can act freely during the stop.
  · Time Slow – Reduces the speed of targeted enemies or projectiles.
  · Time Acceleration – Increases Ispecula's own speed (movement, attack, reaction) for a short duration.
· Overclock (120% output): Time Stop duration increases to 15 seconds, and Ispecula can selectively allow allies to move during the stop.
· Ultimate (Time mode only): Timestop – A full‑area time freeze that lasts 30 seconds. Ispecula can move and attack, but damage is reduced by 50% during the freeze.

🔥 FIRE MODE

· Symbol: Red flame
· Abilities:
  · Fire Slashes – Ranged slashes of condensed flame that can be spammed.
  · Kitchen Submode – Grants access to Dismantle (rapid, precise slashes) and Cleave (heavy, wide‑arc slashes).
  · World Cutting Slash (WCS) – A vertical slash that cuts dimensions. Requires a brief charge.
  · Universe Cutting Slash (UCS) – Overclocked version of WCS; cuts through universal barriers. Extremely draining.
· Overclock (120% output): Fire Slashes become explosive on impact; WCS charge time halved.
· Ultimate (Fire mode only): World Cutting Slash (standard) or Universe Cutting Slash (overclocked). The slash does not just cut matter — it severs existence.

---

⚡ THUNDER MODE

· Symbol: Yellow lightning bolt
· Abilities:
  · Lightning Bolts – Fires rapid, arcing bolts of electricity.
  · Chain Lightning – A bolt that jumps between multiple enemies.
  · EMP Burst – Disables technology and magical constructs in a large area.
  · Thunder Charge – Self‑buff that adds electrical damage to Ispecula's physical attacks.
· Overclock (120% output): Lightning bolts become hitscan (instant). Chain lightning jumps to twice as many targets.
· Ultimate (Thunder mode only): "Didn't Strike Me Tho" – A massive lightning strike that hits all enemies in a large area. Ispecula is immune to damage during the attack.

---

❄️ ICE MODE

· Symbol: Cyan snowflake
· Abilities:
  · Ice Shards – Rapid, piercing projectiles that can freeze small areas.
  · Frost Armor – Encases Ispecula in a protective shell that reduces damage and freezes melee attackers.
  · Ice Wall – Creates a temporary barrier of solid ice.
  · Snowstorm – Area‑of‑effect freezing storm that slows enemies and deals cold damage over time.
· Overclock (120% output): Ice Shards become larger and travel faster; Frost Armor reflects 50% of melee damage back.
· Ultimate (Ice mode only): Absolute Zero – Drops the temperature in a large area to absolute zero, freezing enemies solid and shattering weaker ones instantly.

🪐 SPACE MODE

  · Fluid Form – Ispecula partially liquefies, reducing physical damage taken for a short duration.

🔄 ADAPTATION MODE (Special)

· Not a standard mode; activated separately via the eyepatch.
· Effect: The eyepatch shatters, revealing a cycling, multicolored eye. The ☸️ dharma wheel appears above Ispecula's head with a slight delay.

  · Resistance stacking – Gains 10 stacks per hit taken. At 100 stacks (10 hits), becomes immune to that specific damage type.
  · Duration – 5 minutes (300 AE, drains 1 per second).
  · Cooldown – Requires AE to recharge; eyepatch reforms when depleted.
  · Risk – Stacks reset on death. Using Adaptation Mode leaves Ispecula exhausted afterward.
